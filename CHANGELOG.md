## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-28

### Added

- Initial public-ready standalone Agent Skill.
- User-specified or inferred knowledge-category routing.
- Existing-document merge and focused new-category workflow.
- Default review mode with complete preview and explicit approval.
- Per-request direct-write mode for users who explicitly waive preview and confirmation.
- Preservation of already-conclusive source text without forced compression.
- Privacy pass, path-boundary checks, and untrusted-note handling.
- Obsidian desktop prerequisite and official download guidance.
- English and Simplified Chinese documentation.
- Fictional usage and preview examples.
